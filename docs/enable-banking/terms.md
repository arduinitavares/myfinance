# MyFinance Personal Research terms of use

Last updated: 16 September 2026

## Personal use

MyFinance Personal Research is a private, non-commercial research application for its operator's own bank accounts. It is not offered to the public or used to access other people's accounts. These terms describe its Enable Banking account-information connection.

## Account access

The account holder chooses which accounts to authorize. The connection is used to retrieve account information, balances, and transactions for local recordkeeping and analysis, with a research focus on 2026. It is not used to initiate payments or transfers.

The operator must use the connection within the bank's and Enable Banking's applicable permissions and terms. The operator keeps application credentials and private keys confidential.

## Data and results

Information may be delayed, incomplete, or corrected by the source. Local analyses depend on the information supplied and do not replace the bank's statements or records. The operator checks discrepancies against the source records.

## Ending use

The account holder may stop using the application and revoke its access through the bank or Enable Banking. Revocation and deletion are separate actions: previously downloaded local records and backups remain until the operator deletes them.

## Contact

The operator's contact address is the data-protection email shown in this application's Enable Banking consent screen. The accompanying privacy notice explains how the research data is handled.
