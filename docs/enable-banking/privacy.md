# MyFinance Personal Research privacy notice

Last updated: 16 September 2026

## Purpose and scope

MyFinance Personal Research is a private application operated by its account holder for non-commercial analysis of their own financial information. This notice covers its Enable Banking account-information connection. It is not a service offered to other people.

## Information accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application retrieves information made available by their bank through Enable Banking. This may include account identifiers, account names, currencies, balances, and transactions, including dates, amounts, descriptions, and counterparty details supplied by the bank. The research focuses on transactions in calendar year 2026 and balance observations collected during the research.

## Processing and storage

Banks and Enable Banking process information to provide the authorized connection under their own policies. Information retrieved by this application is stored in local files and a local database under the operator's control. Any copies or backups made by the operator remain their responsibility.

The application does not sell financial information or use it for advertising. Publishing this notice does not publish bank records, account identifiers, credentials, or private keys.

## Retention and control

The operator keeps the research records until they choose to delete them. Deleting retained information includes removing relevant local files and any backup copies. Revoking bank access stops future authorized retrieval but does not automatically erase information already downloaded.

The account holder can review or revoke access through the bank's consent controls or Enable Banking's [data-sharing consent portal](https://enablebanking.com/data-sharing-consents/).

## Contact and provider information

The operator's contact address is the data-protection email shown in this application's Enable Banking consent screen.

Enable Banking's handling of personal information is described in its [Privacy Notice](https://enablebanking.com/privacy).
