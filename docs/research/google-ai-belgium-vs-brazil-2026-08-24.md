# Google AI Belgium vs Brazil — 2026-08-24

## Decision summary

Moving to Brazil can justify changing the Google country/payment profile, but Brazil is **not automatically cheaper in EUR** for every plan. Google AI Ultra includes the individual YouTube Premium plan in **both Belgium and Brazil**; Google AI Pro includes only YouTube Premium Lite, not full Premium.

At the ECB 2026-08-24 reference rate of **€1 = R$6.0068**, the current standard monthly prices compare as follows:

| Plan | Belgium | Brazil | Brazil in EUR | Cheaper region |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Google AI Plus | €4.99 | R$24.99 | about €4.16 | Brazil by about €0.83 |
| Google AI Pro | €21.99 | R$96.99 | about €16.15 | Brazil by about €5.84 |
| Google AI Ultra 5x | €99.99 | R$779.90 | about €129.84 | Belgium by about €29.85 |
| Google AI Ultra 20x | €219.99 | R$999.90 | about €166.46 | Brazil by about €53.53 |

Therefore, Brazil is cheaper for Plus, Pro, and Ultra 20x, but **Belgium is cheaper for Ultra 5x** before card/FX fees. Full YouTube Premium is included in both countries' Ultra plans, so it does not change the Belgium-versus-Brazil comparison for the same Ultra tier.

Sources: [Google Gemini Belgium subscriptions](https://gemini.google/be/subscriptions/?hl=nl), [Google Gemini Brazil subscriptions](https://gemini.google/br/subscriptions/?hl=pt-BR), [ECB reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html).

## YouTube Premium benefit

- **AI Ultra:** full, individual YouTube Premium (ad-free YouTube and YouTube Music, offline and background playback), where available. Google’s availability list includes **Belgium and Brazil**.
- **AI Pro:** YouTube Premium Lite only, where available. Lite is not equivalent: some music/content can still have ads and Lite does not provide the full Premium offline/background experience.
- The benefit is **individual**, not a YouTube Premium family plan. Google says some AI Ultra benefits can be shared with a Google family, but the listed YouTube benefit itself is the individual plan; do not assume family coverage.

Sources: [Google AI plans comparison](https://one.google.com/intl/pt-BR_br/about/google-ai-plans/), [Google One AI Ultra benefits and country list](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/16286513?hl=pt-BR), [YouTube Premium availability and travel policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6307365?hl=en).

## Country/profile and transition constraints

Google says a legal country cannot be edited on an existing payments profile. After moving, create a new Brazil payments profile; new purchases use it, while current services remain tied to the old profile. For Google Play country, Google requires that you are physically in the new country and have a payment method from it; the profile can take up to 48 hours to update. Current subscriptions generally remain active on the old profile unless cancelled; to use a subscription with the new country/profile, cancel the old subscription and resubscribe. Country changes can affect access to some content, Play balance, points, family-group eligibility, and available subscriptions.

Sources: [Google Payments: change legal country](https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/7644076?hl=en), [Google Play: change country](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7431675?hl=en).

## Practical recommendation

- Do not buy Ultra solely to replace a standalone YouTube Premium subscription: even the least expensive Ultra option costs far more than YouTube Premium alone.
- If Google AI Pro is enough and YouTube Premium Lite is acceptable, Brazil Pro is materially cheaper than Belgium Pro. Lite does not replace full YouTube Music Premium.
- If full YouTube Premium is required with Google AI, compare the exact Ultra tier: keep Belgium billing for Ultra 5x, but Brazil billing is cheaper for Ultra 20x at the current rate.
- After the actual move, Brazilian standalone YouTube Premium may be the lowest-cost route if the main goal is YouTube rather than high-limit Google AI. Confirm its live price at checkout because Google does not publish a stable unsigned price table in the sources used here.

## Price caveats

The Google pages mark offers as subject to terms. Treat the displayed prices above as current standard/list prices, not a guaranteed promotional price. Before confirming, check the final checkout screen, billing date, taxes, and whether any introductory offer ends and changes to the standard price. Card-network FX conversion, taxes, and Google billing rules may change the final amount.
