# Consolidated financial record before analytics

MyFinance's product outcome is trustworthy Household expense tracking, supported by a Consolidated Financial Record whose provider evidence retains provenance and whose imported activity is normalized and reconciled. The record exists so internal Transfers, Credit Draws, Credit Card Settlements, and principal moved across countries and currencies cannot corrupt Income or Expense totals, while purchases, loan payments under the Household's practical policy, fees, interest, taxes, penalties, and exchange loss remain visible Expenses. Existing Financial Health, Projection, and Anomaly features remain intact but frozen and deprioritized until Expense Coverage is trustworthy; route optimization, generalized wealth management, and financial advice are outside the core outcome.
