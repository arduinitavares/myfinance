# Balance-sheet-aware financial record

The Consolidated Financial Record models Asset Accounts and Liability Accounts where needed so Credit Draws, Transfers, Credit Card Purchases, Credit Card Settlements, Income, Expenses, and Financial Costs remain distinct. Credit-card funding and overdraft use do not become Income; itemized card purchases become Expenses once, and one or more later settlements reconcile the card statement without duplicating them. Settlement Coverage reports unpaid, partial, full, or overpaid status and the remaining difference; the first milestone treats loan payments as Expenses under the Household's practical tracking policy.
